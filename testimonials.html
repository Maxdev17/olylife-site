The Science Behind It

This isn't a new concept — PEMF has been studied and used in various therapeutic contexts for decades, with research pointing to its role in supporting circulation, reducing inflammation, and aiding the body's natural recovery processes. Terahertz technology works alongside this, using a specific range of the electromagnetic spectrum to gently stimulate tissue and support cellular activity.
